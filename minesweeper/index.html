How to Play Minesweeper

🎯 Objective

Uncover all the safe cells on the board without detonating any hidden mines!

🖱️ Controls

  • Left Click / Tap: Uncover a cell.
  • Right Click / Long Press: Plant or remove a Flag 🚩 to mark suspected mines.
  • Middle Click / Double Click on Revealed Number: "Chord" to automatically uncover all surrounding non-flagged cells if you have flagged the exact number of neighboring mines!
  • Flag Mode Toggle: Enable for easy one-tap flagging on mobile devices.

🔢 Numbers Explained

The number on a revealed cell indicates how many mines are hiding in the 8 adjacent neighboring cells (including diagonals).

💡 Smart Features

• Safe First Click: Your very first click is guaranteed to open a safe 0-mine area!

• Smart Hint 💡: Stressed out? Click Hint to get a safe move calculated by logic algorithms.
